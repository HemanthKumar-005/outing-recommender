### 4. Matrix Factorization (TruncatedSVD / iALS)
* **Algorithmic Engine**:
  * Implemented `matrix_factorization_scores()` in [`services/recommendation-engine/app/scoring.py`](./services/recommendation-engine/app/scoring.py).
  * Constructs a sparse user-item interaction matrix from interactions and applies TruncatedSVD dimensionality reduction into a dense $k$-dimensional latent space ($k=8$).
  * Computes cosine similarity between user and candidate place latent representations, surfacing serendipitous cross-category recommendations that share latent affinity without direct category or tag overlap.
* **Integration & Blending**:
  * Blended latent SVD scores with explicit co-occurrence collaborative filtering in [`services/recommendation-engine/app/main.py`](./services/recommendation-engine/app/main.py).

### 5. Offline Evaluation Harness & 5-Fold Cross-Validation
* **Evaluation Framework**:
  * Upgraded [`scripts/evaluate.py`](./scripts/evaluate.py) to connect to PostgreSQL and extract real interaction sequences.
  * Implemented 5-Fold Cross-Validation protocol across:
    * **Precision@K** ($K=5$)
    * **Recall@K** ($K=5$)
    * **NDCG@K** ($K=5$)
    * **Mean Average Precision (MAP@K)**
    * **Intra-List Diversity (ILD@K)**: Measures pairwise cosine dissimilarity among top-K recommendations to ensure diverse recommendations.
